OTHER HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND AREAS
Heavy Rainfall Outlook
Refer to Weather Advisory No. 9 issued at 5:00 PM today for the heavy rainfall outlook due to the TC ROSAL.
Severe Winds
The wind signals warn the public of the general wind threat over an area due to the tropical cyclone. Local winds may be
slightly stronger/enhanced in coastal and upland/mountainous areas exposed to winds. Winds are less strong in areas sheltered
from the prevailing wind direction.
 Minor to moderate impacts from gale-force winds are possible within any of the areas under Wind Signal No. 2.
 Minimal to minor impacts from strong winds are possible within any of the areas under Wind Signal No. 1.
The highest Wind Signal that may be raised during the passage of ROSAL is Wind Signal No. 3, which is likely to be
hoisted over Batanes. However, if ROSAL will move a more north northwestward track, hoisting of Wind Signal No. 3 will
become less likely.
The Northeasterly Windflow and the periphery of ROSAL will bring strong to gale-force gusts over the following areas (especially
in coastal and upland areas exposed to winds):
 Today (22 October): Most of Luzon and Visayas, Zamboanga Peninsula, BARMM, Northern Mindanao, Davao Region,
Agusan del Norte, Surigao del Norte, Dinagat Islands, and Sarangani.
 Friday (23 October): Most of Luzon and Visayas, Davao Region, Zamboanga del Norte, Misamis Occidental, and
Camiguin.
 Saturday (24 October): Batanes, Babuyan Islands, Ilocos Norte, Polillo Islands, Bicol Region, Romblon, Capiz, Iloilo,
and Negros Occidental.

HAZARDS AFFECTING COASTAL WATERS
A Gale Warning is in effect over the seaboard of Northern Luzon and the eastern seaboards of Central and Southern
Luzon. For more details, refer to Gale Warning No. 5 issued at 5:00 PM today.
24-Hour Sea Condition Outlook
Up to very rough, high, or very high seas over the following coastal waters:
 Up to 12.0 m: The northern and eastern seaboards of Batanes.
 Up to 9.0 m: The seaboard of Babuyan Islands; the remaining seaboard of Batanes; the northeastern seaboard of
mainland Cagayan.
 Up to 7.0 m: The remaining seaboard of mainland Cagayan; the seaboard of northern Isabela.
 Up to 6.0 m: The northern seaboard of Ilocos Norte.
 Up to 5.5 m: The remaining seaboards of Ilocos Norte and Isabela.
 Up to 4.5 m: The seaboards of Ilocos Sur and northern Aurora; the northern and eastern seaboards of Polillo Islands and
Catanduanes; the northern seaboards of Camarines Norte and Camarines Sur.
 Sea travel is risky for all types or tonnage of vessels. All mariners must remain in port or, if underway, seek
shelter or safe harbor as soon as possible until winds and waves subside.
Up to rough seas over the following coastal waters:
 Up to 4.0 m: The seaboard of northern La Union; the western seaboard of Pangasinan; the northern seaboard of Northern
Samar.
 Up to 3.5 m: The seaboard of Northern Quezon; the remaining seaboard of Aurora; the eastern seaboards of Albay,
Sorsogon, Northern Samar, and Eastern Samar.
 Up to 3.0 m: The seaboard of Zambales; the eastern seaboards of Dinagat Islands and Siargao-Bucas Grande Islands.
 Mariners of small seacrafts, including all types of motorbancas, are advised not to venture out to sea under these
conditions, especially if inexperienced of operating ill-equipped vessels.
Up to moderate to rough seas over the following coastal waters:
 Up to 2.5 m: The western seaboards of Bataan, Lubang Islands, and Surigao del Sur; the remaining seaboards of La
Union, Pangasinan, and Catanduanes.
 Up to 2.0 m: The western seaboards of Batangas, Cavite, and Calamian Islands; the eastern seaboard of Davao Oriental;
the remaining seaboard of Lubang Islands.
 Mariners of motorbancas and similarly sized vessels are advised to take precautionary measures while venturing
out to sea and, if possible, avoid navigation under these conditions.
Other coastal waters have forecast wave heights of less than 2.0 m.

TRACK AND INTENSITY OUTLOOK
 It must be emphasized that heavy rainfall and severe winds may still be experienced in localities outside the landfall
point and the forecast confidence cone. Refer to “Other Hazards affecting Land Areas” for more details. Furthermore,
the track may still shift within the limit of the forecast confidence cone.
 ROSAL will continue moving generally northwestward until tomorrow (23 October) morning. It will begin to turn north
northwestward by tomorrow afternoon before recurving north northeastward by Saturday (24 October). On the forecast track,
the tropical cyclone will be closest to Extreme Northern Luzon between this afternoon and tomorrow morning. It will exit the
Philippine Area of Responsibility (PAR) by tomorrow evening or Saturday early morning.
 ROSAL is forecast to weaken gradually within 24 hours. By Saturday, this tropical cyclone will begin to weaken rapidly and
may be downgraded into a severe tropical storm.
