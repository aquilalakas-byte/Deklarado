NENENG WEAKENED INTO A LOW PRESSURE AREA AND EXITED THE PHILIPPINE AREA OF RESPONSIBILITY
Location of Center (4:00 PM)
The center of the Low Pressure Area (formerly “NENENG”) was
estimated based on all available data at 480 km North of Itbayat,
Batanes (25.1°N, 122.3°E).
Intensity
Central pressure of 1004 hPa
Present Movement
Almost Stationary
OTHER HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND AREAS
Severe Winds
The Southwest Monsoon will bring strong to gale-force gusts over the following areas (especially in coastal and upland areas
exposed to winds):
 Today until Saturday (22 August): Most of Luzon and Visayas, Davao Region, Northern Mindanao, and Zamboanga
del Norte.
TROPICAL CYCLONE WIND SIGNALS (TCWS) IN EFFECT
No Wind Signal is currently hoisted
HAZARDS AFFECTING COASTAL WATERS
24-Hour Sea Condition Outlook
Up to rough seas over the following coastal waters:
 Up to 4.0 m: The western seaboards of Batanes and Babuyan Islands.
 Up to 3.5 m: The seaboards of Ilocos Norte; the remaining seaboards of Batanes.
 Up to 3.0 m: The seaboards of Ilocos Sur and Zambales; the western seaboard of Pangasinan; the northwestern
seaboard of Kalayaan Islands.
 Mariners of motorbancas and similarly sized vessels are advised to take precautionary measures while
venturing out to sea and, if possible, avoid navigation under these conditions.
Up to moderate seas over the following coastal waters:
 Up to 2.5 m: The seaboards of La Union and Lubang Islands; the northern seaboard of Pangasinan; the western
seaboard of Batangas; the western and southern seaboards of Bataan.
 Up to 2.0 m: The western seaboard of Occidental Mindoro; the northern and western seaboards of northern Palawan
including Calamian Islands; the remaining seaboards of Babuyan Islands and Kalayaan Islands.
 Mariners of motorbancas and similarly sized vessels are advised to take precautionary measures while
venturing out to sea and, if possible, avoid navigation under these conditions.
Other coastal waters have forecast wave heights of less than 2.0 m.
